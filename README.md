## 🎬 Core Features & Capabilities

### 1. Flagship 10-Second Cinematic Shots Standard
- All cinematic shots are standardized to **10-second continuous camera movements** (with 5s, 6s, and 8s overrides).
- **Deliberate Camera Kinetics:** Dolly In/Out, Lateral Glide, Diagonal Push, Orbital Wrap, Vertical Jib Reveal, Parallax Drift, Hero Hold.

### 2. Strict Architectural Preservation Engine
- **Preservation Clause:** Prompts strictly enforce 100% fidelity to wall geometries, interior materials, furniture placement, and vanishing perspectives without AI structural hallucinations.
- **Negative Prompt Safeguards:** Blocks architectural morphing, warping walls, sliding furniture, and jittery camera movements.

### 3. Multi-Provider Video Generation Abstraction
- Provider-agnostic orchestration supporting:
  - **KGM Native Neural Engine** (Local FFmpeg GPU Master)
  - **Runway Gen-3 Alpha**
  - **Luma Dream Machine**
  - **Kling AI v1.5 Pro**
  - **Google Veo 2**

### 4. Server-Side FFmpeg Media Assembly Pipeline
- Full multi-aspect mastering:
  - **16:9 4K / 1080p Cinema Master:** Landscape broadcast video for YouTube, websites, and luxury TV displays.
  - **9:16 Portrait Social Reel:** Vertical video for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
  - **1:1 Square Feed:** Square format for LinkedIn executive announcements and Instagram feeds.
  - **WhatsApp Fast Mobile:** Highly optimized compressed file format for instant broker-to-client mobile distribution.

### 5. Multi-Lingual AI Voiceover & Royalty-Safe Soundstage
- Curated luxury classical, ambient electronic, and cinematic orchestral tracks.
- Multi-lingual voiceover script generation and synthesis in **English**, **Arabic**, and **French**.

### 6. Public Client-Facing Film Presentation Portal (`/film/[publicId]`)
- Bespoke luxury viewing portal for ultra-high-net-worth clients and investors.
- Direct multi-format download selector.
- VIP private viewing and consultation booking form with CRM webhook synchronization.
